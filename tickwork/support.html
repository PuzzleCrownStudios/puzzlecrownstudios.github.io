How to Play

  • Draw circles around the movement to wind it. Every click of the ratchet fills the power reserve, and a tap winds a little too.
  • While there is power the watch ticks, and every tick earns. A freshly wound watch swings wider and earns more, so wind a little now and then.
  • Buy from the order sheet at the bottom: the next part, the next jewel, or a longer mainspring. Each part appears on the movement.
  • Once the regulator is fitted, slide it until the trace on the timing machine runs level: a watch that is on time earns up to three times as much.
  • When every part and jewel is fitted, case the watch. It goes into your watch box and keeps earning while it is wound, and the next caliber lies on the bench.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I earn while I am away?

Yes, for as long as the power reserve lasts: a watch with a two-hour reserve runs and earns for two hours after you close the app, then it stops. A longer mainspring makes it run longer, up to eight hours. When you come back you can collect what it earned, or watch an optional video to double it and wind every watch.

Why has my watch stopped?

Its mainspring has run down. Draw circles around the movement to wind it again. Watches in the watch box stop too: open the box and press Wind, or fit a rotor so a watch winds itself while you play.

What do the jewels do?

Jewels cut friction, so a freshly wound watch swings wider. Every jewel raises what a tick earns just after winding, and a watch needs all of its jewels before it can be cased.

How do I turn off vibration or notifications?

Open Settings (the gear icon) and use the Vibration and Notifications switches.

Are there ads?

Only optional reward videos. There are no banners or pop-up ads, and a video only plays when you choose to watch one for a bonus.

I can't hear anything.

Check that Sound and Music are on in Settings (the gear icon), that your volume is up and that your device is not on silent. Tickwork is best with sound on.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in US states with a privacy law, open Settings in the app and tap Privacy options. Tracking is controlled in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Will I lose my progress if I switch devices?

Progress is saved on your device, not in the cloud. It moves with you when you restore a device backup to your new device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
