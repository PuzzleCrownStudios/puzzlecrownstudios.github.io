How to Play

  • Each board hides groups of four words that share a link.
  • Tap four words, then tap Submit. A correct group locks in and shows its link.
  • A wrong guess costs one mistake. "One away" means three of your four words belong together.
  • Colors run from easy (mint) to tricky (rose). Watch out for red herrings!

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the daily puzzle change?

At midnight in your local time zone. Solve it each day to grow your streak.

I'm stuck. Can I get a hint?

Yes. Tap Reveal a group to solve the easiest remaining group with coins. If you're short on coins, you can watch an optional video instead.

How do I replay the tutorial?

Open Settings (the gear icon on the home screen) and tap Replay tutorial.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, open Settings in the app and tap Privacy settings. For tracking, go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Will I lose my progress if I switch phones?

Progress is saved on your device and included in your iPhone backup. Restoring a backup to a new iPhone brings it with you. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I turn off reminders?

Go to iOS Settings → Notifications → Four Links.
