How to Play

  • Tap the car to earn gold.
  • When you have enough gold, buy the next part in the Parts tab, then tap the car to fit it: wheels, engine, hood, doors, glass, paint and a final polish.
  • When every part is fitted, the car is restored. It goes on show in your gallery and earns gold from then on, and the next car is towed in.
  • Buy businesses, such as an oil change bay or a tire shop, in the Businesses tab. They earn gold every second.
  • There are 12 cars to restore, from a barn-find hatchback to a scissor-door hypercar.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I earn while I'm away?

Yes. Your businesses and gallery keep earning for up to 8 hours while the app is closed; after that the safe is full. When you come back, you can collect the earnings or watch an optional video to double them.

Are there ads?

Only optional reward videos. There are no banners or pop-up ads, and a video only plays when you choose to watch one for a bonus.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Open Settings in the game (the gear icon) and switch Notifications on or off. You can also change them in iOS Settings → Notifications → Mechanic Clicker. The game only sends a few reminders, for example when your safe is full, and never between 22:00 and 08:00.

I can't hear anything.

Check that Sound is on in Settings in the game, that your volume is up and that your device isn't on silent.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), open Settings in the game and tap Ad privacy choices. Tracking is controlled in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Why do the cars have funny names?

All car names in the game are parodies. No real makes or models appear.

Will I lose my progress if I switch phones?

Progress is saved on your device, not in the cloud. It moves with you when you restore a device backup to your new iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

What languages does the game support?

28 languages. The game follows your device language; you can also choose a language for Mechanic Clicker alone in iOS Settings → Apps → Mechanic Clicker → Language.
