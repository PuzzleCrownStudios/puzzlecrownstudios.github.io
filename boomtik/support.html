How to Play

  • The machine plays a loop of sixteen steps. Every step that fires plays its sound and pays you.
  • Tap one of the small pads at the bottom to pick an instrument (the tap plays it and pays, too). The sixteen big pads are its steps: tap one to switch it on or off. Each instrument has a number of steps to place; a step can always be moved.
  • Everything with a price is a control on the machine and lights up when you can afford it: LEVEL UP and +1 STEP for the selected instrument, the locked pads for new instruments, TEMPO, RADIO and BARS.
  • Hold DROP to build up for up to four bars, then let go on the first beat of a bar. On the beat pays for the bars you built, squared; off the beat keeps a fifth. DROP wakes up when the kit has five instruments and needs eight bars of rest after each use.
  • When the kit has all ten instruments, release the record. It goes on your shelf, your fans multiply, and a new kit starts in the next genre.
  • While the app is closed your beat is on the radio and goes on earning for a few hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I earn while I am away?

Yes. Your beat is on the radio for 3 hours while the app is closed, and up to 10 hours once you have turned the RADIO control up. The radio pays a small share of what the beat makes while you play. When you come back you can collect it, or watch an optional video to double it.

I cannot hear anything.

The game is its sound, and an iPhone with the silent switch on plays nothing. Check the switch on the side of the phone and the volume, and that Sound and Music are on in Settings (the gear icon).

Why can I not place another step?

Every instrument has a limited number of steps per bar (shown as Steps 4 / 4). Switch one off to move it, or buy +1 STEP.

What are the glowing pads?

Hot steps. For about twenty seconds a hit on one of them pays double, so it is worth moving a step there.

What happens when I release a record?

The track goes on your shelf with a sleeve drawn from your own pattern, your fans are multiplied by 30 (every hit pays that much more), and you start a new kit in the next genre. Coins and the RADIO control are kept; instruments, levels and tempo start over with the new kit.

How do I turn off vibration or notifications?

Open Settings (the gear icon) and use the Vibration and Notifications switches.

Are there ads?

Only optional reward videos. There are no banners or pop-up ads, and a video only plays when you choose to watch one for a bonus.

I can't hear anything.

Check that Sound and Music are on in Settings (the gear icon), that your volume is up and that your device is not on silent. Boomtik is best with sound on.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in US states with a privacy law, open Settings in the app and tap Privacy options. Tracking is controlled in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Will I lose my progress if I switch devices?

Progress is saved on your device, not in the cloud. It moves with you when you restore a device backup to your new device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
