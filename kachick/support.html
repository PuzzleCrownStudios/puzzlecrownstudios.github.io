How to Play

  • Watch the light meter needle beside the viewfinder and press the camera's shutter button when it is in the red zone: that is a perfect frame, worth three good ones.
  • Wind on before the next frame: swipe the lever through its arc, or just tap it.
  • A full roll goes to the darkroom and develops by itself, also while the app is closed. Open the contact sheet when it is ready to collect what the frames are worth.
  • Now and then something enters the scene. A well exposed frame of it is a keeper: it pays far more and goes into your album, where it makes every frame worth more.
  • Spend coins on your kit (lens, camera body, longer rolls, film stocks, a bigger bag, an assistant) and in the darkroom (more tanks, faster chemistry).
  • Open new places from the map pin at the top. Each needs three album photos from the place before it, and coins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I earn while I am away?

Yes. The darkroom keeps developing the rolls it has, and your assistant shoots the film in the bag for up to 8 hours. When you come back you can collect the finished contact sheets, or watch an optional video to double them.

Why does a roll take so long?

Film from places further away takes longer to develop: five minutes on Home Street, hours in the Desert. More tanks develop more rolls at once, better chemistry shortens the time, and once in a while you can rush a tank with an optional video.

My bag is full. Did I lose a roll?

The camera never stops working. When every tank is busy and the bag is full, the bag keeps its most valuable rolls and lets the weakest one go.

How do I catch the rare subjects?

They turn up while you are shooting yourself, never for the assistant. The needle moves faster while one is in the frame: wait for the red zone and press.

How do I turn off vibration or notifications?

Open Settings (the gear icon) and use the Vibration and Notifications switches.

Are there ads?

Only optional reward videos. There are no banners or pop-up ads, and a video only plays when you choose to watch one for a bonus.

I can't hear anything.

Check that Sound and Music are on in Settings (the gear icon), that your volume is up and that your device is not on silent. Kachick is best with sound on.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in US states with a privacy law, open Settings in the app and tap Privacy options. Tracking is controlled in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Will I lose my progress if I switch devices?

Progress is saved on your device, not in the cloud. It moves with you when you restore a device backup to your new device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
