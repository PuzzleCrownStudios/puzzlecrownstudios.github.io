How to Play

  • Tap the rock to chisel it. Strike the glowing fault line for a clean hit, and keep hitting it for a growing streak.
  • When the rock splits you see what was inside. The first specimen of every mineral goes into your cabinet, and each one makes all finds worth more.
  • Spend coins in the workshop: sharpen your chisel, hire an apprentice and buy machines, and a loupe for better odds of rare minerals.
  • Open new dig sites from the map pin at the top. Each needs five minerals from the site before it, and coins.
  • Machines keep working while the app is closed, and set promising geodes aside for you. Promising geodes are never common.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I earn while I am away?

Yes. Your machines keep cracking for up to 8 hours while the app is closed. When you come back you can collect what they made, or watch an optional video to double it.

What is a promising geode?

A geode with gold flecks that always holds a rare, epic or legendary mineral. Your machines set one aside every few hours while you are away (up to three), and one turns up every few minutes while you play.

How do I find the legendary minerals?

Every rock has a small chance, and promising geodes a much better one. The loupe in the workshop raises the odds of everything better than common.

How do I turn off vibration or notifications?

Open Settings (the gear icon) and use the Vibration and Notifications switches.

Are there ads?

Only optional reward videos. There are no banners or pop-up ads, and a video only plays when you choose to watch one for a bonus.

I can't hear anything.

Check that Sound and Music are on in Settings (the gear icon), that your volume is up and that your device is not on silent. Tink is best with sound on.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in US states with a privacy law, open Settings in the app and tap Privacy options. Tracking is controlled in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Will I lose my progress if I switch devices?

Progress is saved on your device, not in the cloud. It moves with you when you restore a device backup to your new device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
