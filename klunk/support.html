How to Play

  • Tap the big knife switch at the bottom of the console. Every throw adds energy to your bank.
  • When a lamp lights, the control next to it needs you, and a yellow tag says how: flick a tripped breaker up, turn a dial until the gauge is back in the green, slide a fader to its lit mark, pull a lever right down, or lift a cover and press the button.
  • Calls answered in a row raise the tune meter, up to twice the output while you are at the console. A call you ignore goes out by itself after a few seconds and only ends the streak.
  • Open Work orders to level up modules, bolt new ones into the empty bays, upgrade the charge switch and the fuel tank, and fit regulators.
  • A regulator answers its module's lamps by itself and keeps part of the module running while the app is closed. When every module of a plant has one, you can commission the plant: it goes on the wall as a certificate and the next plant opens.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I earn while I am away?

Yes. Modules with a regulator keep 20% to 45% of their output running while the app is closed, for as long as the fuel tank lasts: 3 hours at first, up to 8 hours with upgrades. When you come back you can collect the shift report, or watch an optional video to double it.

What happens if I miss a lamp?

Nothing breaks and nothing is taken away. The lamp goes out after about twelve seconds and your streak starts again from one.

How do I commission a plant?

Install all six modules, fit a regulator on each of them, then pay the commissioning fee at the bottom of Work orders. Every certificate adds 50% to all output for good.

What are the numbers along the top of the console?

One tile for each of the six bays. A tile lights in the lamp colour when its module has a call. Tap a tile to bring that bay in front of you.

How do I turn off vibration or notifications?

Open Settings (the gear icon) and use the Vibration and Notifications switches.

Are there ads?

Only optional reward videos. There are no banners or pop-up ads, and a video only plays when you choose to watch one for a bonus.

I can't hear anything.

Check that Sound and Music are on in Settings (the gear icon), that your volume is up and that your device is not on silent. Klunk is best with sound on.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in US states with a privacy law, open Settings in the app and tap Privacy options. Tracking is controlled in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Will I lose my progress if I switch devices?

Progress is saved on your device, not in the cloud. It moves with you when you restore a device backup to your new device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
