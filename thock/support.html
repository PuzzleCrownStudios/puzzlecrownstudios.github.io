How to Play

  • Tap any key to make it thock. Every press earns thocks, and you can tap with several fingers at once.
  • Spend thocks to install keycaps one by one. Every installed key auto-types and earns for you.
  • Upgrade your switches and add mods such as lube, foam and tape. Each one changes how your board sounds and feels.
  • Tap on the beat of the music to build a combo multiplier, and catch golden keys for bonuses and Frenzy mode.
  • When your board is finished, launch a Group Buy to start a bigger board with a permanent Hype bonus and a new keycap set.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I earn while I'm away?

Yes. Your keys keep auto-typing for up to 3 hours while the app is closed. When you come back, you can collect the earnings or watch an optional video to double them.

What does a Group Buy reset?

Keys, switches and mods reset, and you start a new, bigger board. You keep your Hype (a permanent income bonus) and every keycap set you've unlocked.

Are there ads?

Only optional reward videos. There are no banners or pop-up ads, and a video only plays when you choose to watch one for a bonus.

I can't hear anything.

Check that Sound effects and Music are on in Settings (the gear icon), that your volume is up and that your phone isn't on silent. Thock is best with sound on, ideally with headphones.

How do I turn off vibration or the auto-typing sounds?

Open Settings and switch off Haptics or Auto-typing sounds.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, open Settings in the app and tap Privacy options. On iPhone, tracking is controlled in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Will I lose my progress if I switch phones?

Progress is saved on your device, not in the cloud. It moves with you when you restore a device backup to your new phone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I start over?

Open Settings and tap Reset progress. This can't be undone.
